Good to Know

Before your appointment

A few things to keep in mind so your service goes smoothly and results last as long as possible.

01

Come with Clean Skin

Arrive with your face free of makeup, oil, and lotion around the brow and eye area.

02

Skip the Caffeine

Avoid caffeine before lash appointments to reduce sensitivity and twitching, this will allow for the best outcome.

03

No Contacts for Lashes

Remove contact lenses before lash services and bring glasses instead.

04

Avoid Retinol / Lash Serums

Avoid exfoliating products, retinol, AHAs/BHAs or other strong skincare actives around the brow area for at least 7-14 days before your appointment.

05

Plan for Appointment Length

Appointments typically last between 60-90 minutes depending on the service selected, please plan your day accordingly.

06

Service History

Please let me know before your service about any recent brow treatments or products that may affect your service.

07

Henna Prep

For tint or henna services, a patch test may be recommended 24–48 hours before your appointment.

*Results may vary based on skin type, aftercare and lifestyle.

08

Lash Prep

Please arrive with clean lashes and no eye makeup. Avoid mascara, strip-lash glue and oily products around the eyes before your appointment. If you wear contact lenses, you will be required to remove them before your service.
